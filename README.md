# ABC Consultancy - Overseas Education & Study Abroad Portal

A modern, responsive, and high-converting study abroad consultancy web platform designed for **ABC Consultancy**.

## 🌟 Features
- **Hero & Trust Indicators**: High-impact banner, stats counters, and credential highlights.
- **Country & Program Guides**: Dedicated sections for UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Australia, and Medical/MBBS programs abroad.
- **Admission Eligibility Checker & AI Matcher**: Interactive modal for students to find ideal universities and get instant eligibility estimates.
- **Direct Lead Routing**: Direct integration with WhatsApp (`+91 72919 19145`) and telephone callback requests.
- **Comprehensive Resources**: Visa processing roadmap, cost calculators, test preparation (IELTS/TOEFL/GRE), and FAQ accordion.
- **Glassmorphism & Modern UI**: Tailored with Tailwind CSS and FontAwesome icon suite.
